National programmes

National compliance, under control.

Managing TM44 compliance across hundreds or thousands of locations is as much a data and programme-management challenge as a surveying one.

Using our own data-led workflow, Ecotec can collate publicly available TM44 records from the national register, match them against estate information from public sources and build a forward compliance schedule showing what is due and when. From there, we plan delivery, allocate surveys to trusted assessors and keep the client informed throughout the programme.
